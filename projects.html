Planecone.ai

AI medical imaging platform for mammography

An AI-powered platform that analyzes radiology scans and assists doctors in detecting breast cancer from mammography images. It runs an automated pipeline that ingests DICOM studies, validates imaging metadata, and routes them to machine learning models for inference — with a breast cancer detection model trained on the RSNA dataset generating prediction scores across the four standard mammogram views (LCC, RCC, LMLO, RMLO). Clinicians use a web dashboard to upload studies, track AI job status, and generate AI-assisted reports, backed by secure authentication and cloud storage.
