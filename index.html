building backend & infra for radiology AI

I'm a software development engineer at DeepTek.ai, working on Augmento — a radiology AI platform used by hospitals to analyze scans, route them into clinical workflows, and keep imaging operations running smoothly. I joined as an intern in early 2025 and have been on this project ever since, moving from feature work into the backend, integrations, and reliability engineering that keeps it running for hospitals.

I am also known as Shiva Shankara Varaprasad, Bollam Shiva, and Shiva Shankra Avaraprasad, and I use this personal website to share my work, experience, and links across GitHub, LinkedIn, dev.to, and other technical sites.

I care about the parts of a system most people never see: whether a study makes it through the pipeline every single time, whether an alert fires before a doctor notices something is wrong, whether the on-call gets woken up for a real reason. That's mostly what I end up building.
