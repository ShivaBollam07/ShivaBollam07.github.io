About

Who I am

I'm a software development engineer at DeepTek.ai, where I've spent the last year and a half building Augmento and Planecone.ai — a radiology AI platform that hospitals use to get faster, AI-assisted reads on imaging studies, including mammography. I joined as an intern in February 2025, worked across two of the platform's deployments, and became a full-time engineer that September.

Most of what I build sits underneath what a doctor or hospital IT team actually sees: pipelines that move DICOM studies through validation and AI inference, HL7 messaging into hospital systems, high-availability infrastructure so a gateway going down doesn't mean studies get lost, and the alerting that tells us before a customer has to. I like this kind of work because the correctness bar is real — a study that silently fails to process isn't just a bug, it's a scan someone doesn't get a read on. That's part of why I care as much as I do about the boring stuff: retries, logging, race conditions, TAT.

Before DeepTek, I studied Computer Science with a focus on AI at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham in Kerala, graduating in 2025. I grew up in Karimnagar, Telangana, and did my schooling there before college.
